Setting up payments

  • Create a free Stripe account, then in the Dashboard go to Payment Links → New.
  • Add a product priced at your price per night (£{{price}}), and turn on "Let customers adjust quantity".
  • Paste the generated link above. Guests will be told to set the quantity to their number of nights at checkout.
  • You can leave this blank for now and add it later from Manage Listings.
